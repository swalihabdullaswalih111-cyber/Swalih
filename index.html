About

Design is a craft, and a conversation.

Hi, I'm Abdulla Swalih, a Senior Graphic Designer with over five years of experience in creating impactful visual identities and engaging brand experiences. My expertise includes branding, logo design, social media creatives, print materials, marketing campaigns, and UI concepts. I combine creativity with strategic thinking to transform ideas into meaningful designs that communicate effectively, strengthen brands, and leave a lasting impression. I’m passionate about exploring new ideas and delivering creative solutions that make a real impact.

In the classroom and in the studio I focus on fundamentals: hierarchy, typography, colour and storytelling, supported by tools like Photoshop and Illustrator.
